Terms & Conditions

Last updated: June 2026 · Please read before using Tohfa.

1. Who Can Join

  • Must be an individual or small business selling genuinely handmade or customized gifts.

  • Sellers must maintain an active and authentic Instagram presence for brand verification.

  • Must be based in India and have a valid Indian bank account for payouts.

2. What Sellers Agree To

  • All products listed must be genuinely handmade, hand-finished, or customized by the seller.

  • No fakes, replicas, or mass-manufactured third-party items are permitted on the platform.

  • Sellers are solely responsible for fulfilling every order within the promised lead time.

"Supporting the human hands behind every thoughtful gift."

3. What Tohfa Promises

  • A curated marketplace to list products and reach a niche audience of gifting enthusiasts.

  • Connections with genuine buyers who value the artisanal process.

  • Secure, end-to-end payment handling via our partner Razorpay.

4. If Rules Are Broken

  • Tohfa reserves the right to remove listings that do not meet our quality standards.

  • Immediate account suspension for providing false information or listing inauthentic products.

5. Liability

  • Tohfa is a marketplace facilitator, not the manufacturer or artisan of the products.

  • The seller is fully responsible for product quality, defects, and shipping insurance.

1. What Buyers Agree To

  • All purchases are final once the order is confirmed by the artisan.

  • No cancellations once the handcrafting process has begun.

  • Ensuring correct shipping and contact details are provided at checkout.

2. Payments

  • Secure payment processing handled via Razorpay.

  • Full payment required at the time of order placement.

  • Prices are inclusive of all applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

3. Delivery

  • Timelines vary by artisan as products are often made-to-order.

  • Tohfa is not liable for delays caused by third-party logistics partners.

  • Failed delivery due to incorrect address is the buyer's responsibility.

4. Refunds & Disputes

  • Items are non-refundable unless received in a damaged condition.

  • Disputes must be raised within 48 hours of delivery with photo evidence.

  • Tohfa will provide fair mediation between buyer and seller.

5. What Tohfa Is Not Responsible For

  • Final product quality is the sole responsibility of the individual artisan.

  • Tohfa serves as a marketplace connector to discover artisan stories.
