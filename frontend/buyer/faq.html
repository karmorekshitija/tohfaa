Frequently Asked Questions

Thoughtful Gifting, Made Effortless.

How do I place an order?

add
Simply browse our collection, select your preferred item, and click 'Add to Cart'. Follow the checkout prompts to finalize your purchase.

What payment methods are accepted?

add
We accept all major credit cards, UPI, and digital wallets to ensure a seamless transaction experience.

Can I track my order in real-time?

add
Yes, once your order is dispatched, a tracking link will be sent via email and SMS for real-time monitoring.

What is your return policy?

add
Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in original packaging. Custom items are non-returnable.

Are the products ethically sourced?

add
Yes, every artisan partner on Tohfa is vetted for ethical labor practices and sustainable material usage.

What if my item arrives damaged?

add
Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will arrange a replacement or full refund immediately.

Is my personal information secure?

add
We use bank-level encryption and industry-leading security protocols to protect your personal and financial data.

How do I become a Tohfa artisan?

add
Apply through our 'Artisan Portal' with a portfolio of your work. Our curation team will review and contact you.

What are the platform fees?

add
We charge a nominal commission on successful sales. There are no monthly listing fees for our standard artisan plan.

Do I handle my own shipping?

add
You can choose to use Tohfa's logistics network or manage shipping yourself through verified partners.
